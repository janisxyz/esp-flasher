ESP Flasher writes firmware to ESP8266 and ESP32 boards directly from an Android phone. Plug in a USB-OTG adapter, pick a .bin, and flash. The app speaks the real Espressif ROM bootloader protocol (SLIP + stub-less ROM commands). It is not a timer animation and not a simulator.

Supported chips
• ESP8266
• ESP32, ESP32-S2, ESP32-S3
• ESP32-C3, ESP32-C6, ESP32-H2

USB adapters
• WCH CH340 / CH341
• Silicon Labs CP210x
• FTDI
• CDC ACM
• Native USB on ESP32-S2 / S3 / C3 and related chips

What it does
• Detects the chip and flash size from the ROM bootloader
• Inspects the firmware header (0xE9 magic and ESP-IDF chip id)
• Warns if the image does not match the connected chip — you can still flash anyway
• Writes at the real serial cursor and can verify after write
• Optional erase-before-flash and automatic bootloader reset (DTR/RTS)
• Opens .bin files from Files, Downloads, Drive, or Share / Open with

If automatic reset fails, hold BOOT, reconnect USB, and tap Try again.

Privacy
Flashing happens on the device. Firmware bytes are not uploaded. There is no account, no ads, and no analytics. See the in-listing privacy policy.
