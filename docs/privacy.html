ESP Flasher

Privacy policy

Last updated 20 August 2026

ESP Flasher (“the app”) flashes firmware to Espressif chips from an Android phone over USB. This policy describes what the app does with data.

No collection, no accounts, no ads

The app does not create accounts, does not include ads or analytics SDKs, and does not send firmware, logs, or device identifiers to us or to any third party. There is no server backend for the Android app.

What stays on the device

  • USB access to talk to a connected UART / native-USB adapter.
  • Firmware .bin files you choose. They are read locally and written to the chip. They are not uploaded.
  • Settings such as theme, verify-after-flash, and erase-before-flash, stored in Android DataStore on the phone.
  • Optional screen wake-lock only while a flash is running.

Permissions

  • USB host — required to detect and flash boards.
  • Wake lock — optional, keeps the screen on during a write.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or internet.

Children

The app is a developer tool. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes
